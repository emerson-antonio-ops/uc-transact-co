Movement 06 · Continuous documentation

Evidence becomes valuable when it survives the session

The chat window closes at 23:00. What the room decided has to keep working without it — labeled, reviewable, maintained.

The brief keeps four kinds of statement apart
01 Fact Directly supported by evidence — it cites a path, an object, or a query.
02 Inference Reasonable, but not conclusively established — it travels with its falsifier.
03 Decision Chosen by someone with authority — never invented by the agent.
04 Question Needs more evidence or an accountable owner — gets a name and a next action.

Labels survive summarization. Nothing gets upgraded in the retelling — an inference never becomes a fact by being repeated.
